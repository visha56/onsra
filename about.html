Our Story

A sanctuary born from the belief that yoga, when taught authentically, can transform lives.

Who We Are

ONSRA Yoga Ashram

Nestled in the lush tropical landscape of Goa, ONSRA Yoga Ashram is a sanctuary dedicated to the authentic transmission of traditional yoga. We believe yoga is far more than physical exercise — it is a complete science of life, a path toward self-discovery, and a practice that, when rooted in tradition, has the power to fundamentally transform the practitioner.

Founded in the spirit of the ancient Guru-Shishya (teacher-student) tradition, ONSRA has welcomed hundreds of students from across the globe — from complete beginners seeking a healthy lifestyle shift, to experienced practitioners ready to deepen their path and become teachers themselves.

We are proud to be Yoga Alliance USA registered, offering a 200-Hour Teacher Training Course that holds the highest international standards while remaining rooted in the authenticity of India's yogic heritage.

Our Belief

Our Yogic Philosophy

At ONSRA, we are guided by the classical teachings of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Bhagavad Gita, and the Hatha Yoga Pradipika. We believe in yoga as a complete system — not just the asana (posture) practice, but the full 8-limbed path of Ashtanga Yoga:

Yama & Niyama — Ethical foundations
Asana — Physical postures and stability
Pranayama — Mastery of life force
Pratyahara — Withdrawal of senses
Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi — Meditation states
"Yoga is the journey of the self, through the self, to the self."
— Bhagavad Gita
