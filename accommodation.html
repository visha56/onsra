Stay at ONSRA Ashram

Comfortable ashram rooms, organic meals, and the healing energy of Goa.

Ashram Living

Your Home for 24 Days

Our ashram accommodation is designed to support your practice — simple, clean, and deeply comfortable in an authentic tropical setting. Staying on campus allows you to be fully immersed in the ashram rhythm without distractions, and creates a shared bond with your fellow students.

All rooms open onto our lush garden. The sound of birds and the breeze through palm trees become your morning alarm. Our organic meals are prepared fresh three times a day by our dedicated kitchen team.

Sattvic Nourishment

Organic Meals Included

Three fresh, organic, vegetarian meals per day — prepared with love and intention.

🌅

Morning Herbal Tea

Start your day with fresh ginger, tulsi or lemongrass tea before morning sadhana.

🥣

Nutritious Breakfast

Porridge, fresh fruits, idli-sambar, or poha — a nourishing start after your morning practice.

🍽

Sattvic Lunch

A wholesome midday meal with dal, rice, sabzi, roti and seasonal vegetables.

🫖

Evening Tea & Snack

Herbal tea with a light snack to sustain you through the afternoon sessions.

🌙

Light Dinner

An early, light dinner of soup, khichdi or similar — easy to digest for a restful night.

🌱

100% Vegetarian

All meals are vegetarian, freshly prepared, and free of onion & garlic during the course.
