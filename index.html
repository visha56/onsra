Our Philosophy

Rooted in Tradition,
Open to the World

ONSRA Yoga Ashram is a sacred space in the heart of Goa, where ancient yogic wisdom meets the transformative power of the ocean. We offer immersive yoga teacher training and retreats for students from across the globe who seek an authentic, traditional experience.

Our 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course follows the classical Hatha Yoga tradition, taught by experienced masters in a living ashram environment — with organic meals, breathwork, philosophy, and community.
