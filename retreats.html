Featured Retreat

"Nourish Her"

Yoga & Women's Wellness Retreat

Theme: Hormonal Balance · Self Love · Self Confidence
🌸 For Women 🗓 3 Days 🌊 Beachside Goa

A sacred, immersive 3-day retreat designed exclusively for women — combining yoga, pranayama, nutrition workshops, nature walks, satsang and community. Each day is thoughtfully crafted to help you arrive, nourish, and integrate.

What's Included in the Retreat

Morning pranayama, beachside vinyasa, and restorative evening practice.

🥗

Nourishing Meals

Organic, sattvic meals crafted for hormonal health and deep nourishment.

Nutrition Workshop

Practical workshops on mindful eating and nutrition for women's wellness.

🎵

Satsang & Music

Evening satsang, devotional music and dance for joy and community.

Nature Walks

Guided nature walks in Goa's lush tropical landscapes for grounding.

Personal Wellness Plan

One-on-one personal wellness planning session to take home with you.
