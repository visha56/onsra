TTC Daily Rhythm

A Typical Day at ONSRA

Each day of the TTC follows a structured ashram schedule designed to deepen your practice, maximize learning, and immerse you in authentic yogic lifestyle. The rhythm of early rising, regular sadhana, and shared meals creates a powerful transformation.

📌 Important Notes:

100% attendance is mandatory for all sessions
Saturday is the designated rest / self-study day
Phones are encouraged to be kept away during practice sessions
Sattvic (vegetarian) diet is followed throughout the course

5:30 – 6:00 AM 🌅 Wake Up & Personal Hygiene
6:00 – 7:00 AM 🧘 Morning Meditation & Pranayama
7:00 – 9:00 AM 🤸 Hatha Yoga Asana Practice
9:00 – 10:00 AM 🥣 Breakfast & Rest

11:30 AM – 1:00 PM 💪 Anatomy & Physiology

6:30 – 7:00 PM 🫖 Herbal Tea Break
7:00 – 8:00 PM 🍽 Dinner
8:00 – 9:00 PM 📿 Satsang / Mantra Chanting / Kirtan
10:00 PM 🌙 Lights Out / Rest
Weekly Structure

24-Day Course Phases

🌱

Week 1 — Foundation

Establishing ashram routine, beginning asana practice, introduction to yoga philosophy and anatomy basics.

🔥

Weeks 2 & 3 — Deepening

Advanced asana, alignment & adjustment practice, pranayama, meditation techniques and teaching methodology.

✨

Week 4 — Integration

Teaching practicums, written exams, practical assessments, and graduation ceremony.
